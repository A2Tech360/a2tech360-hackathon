## Slide 5

LOCAL IMPACT TRACK BRIEF (4 minutes)

"Build something that makes life measurably better for people in Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County."

a2Tech360 spends ten days celebrating what this region invents. This is the part where we invent something.

Every problem worth solving here already belongs to somebody: riders waiting on a route that doesn't quite work, small businesses competing against everything online, nonprofits doing serious work on thin budgets, and students, newcomers, parents, makers, and longtime residents who could help each other if they knew how.

The only requirement: a specific person in this county could use what you build on Monday morning.

Directions, not a menu:
- Getting around: transit, parking, biking, and the last mile
- Main Street: helping local businesses reach the people already walking past them
- Showing up: connecting volunteers, mentors, and donors to the organizations that need them
- Access: food, housing, benefits, childcare, and the gap between a service existing and someone finding it
- Civic clarity: making local data, meetings, permits, and decisions legible to normal humans
- Belonging: helping people find each other and find what's happening

The one rule: ground it in something real. Talk to someone who has the problem. Judges will ask who you built this for, and "everyone" is the wrong answer.

Build requirements: Jaseci/Jac stack (at least 40% Jac), plus Google, IBM, or Baz tooling alongside it. Local Impact projects use Baz for code review.

ACTIVITY (3 minutes): open tracks/local-impact.md in the starter kit and fill in the first three lines of the problem-discovery worksheet: Who, What we heard, The problem in their words. Mentors will come around.

## Slide 6

JUDGING (3 minutes)

The IBM hackathon platform scores each submission on five criteria, 5 points each, 25 total. Final score is the average of the judges' scores.

1. Completeness and feasibility: How feasible is the solution? How fully planned? How complete is the proof of concept? How clear is the application of IBM technology?
2. Creativity and innovation: How unique and original is the approach to applying AI? Is it differentiated?
3. Design and usability: How good is the UX? How quickly could real users adopt it?
4. Effectiveness and efficiency: Does it address a high-priority issue within the theme? Measurable impact? Can it scale?
5. Michigan impact: keeping value, jobs, and opportunity in Michigan. Show a practical application solving a real Michigan challenge, clear business value, a working proof of concept, and strong storytelling that positions Michigan as a technology innovator.

Notice how well the Michigan impact criterion lines up with the Local Impact track. Build for a real person here and you score on both.

JacHacks judges on Devpost also look at: technical execution, use of Jac and Jaseci (by llm(), walkers, graph modeling), creativity, the presentation and demo, impact, and depth of agentic behavior.

Presentations are 5 minutes: 3 to 4 minutes presenting and 1 to 2 minutes of video demo.

Local Impact Grand Prize: the team whose project best combines local relevance, technical execution, and a plausible path to existing after the weekend.
Community Favorite: every participant votes.

## Slide 9

STEP 2: FORM YOUR TEAM ON THE IBM PLATFORM (hands-on, 3 minutes)

Everyone on your team must be registered at ibm.biz/a2tech360. Each person can be on only one team and submit one project.

Team Lead (whoever creates the team is automatically the Lead, and manages the submission):
1. Go to My Team > Create a Team.
2. Enter a team name (4 to 64 characters). Optional: upload a team image you have permission to use, and add an "About your team" description.
3. Click Create Team.

Invite teammates, two ways:
- Know their email? My Team > Team Members > enter it in "Specify email" > Invite.
- Browse: My Team > Invite Members > search or filter > Invite.
Invitees get an email and an on-site notification. Once they accept, they're on the team.

Need teammates or a team?
- Find Members (AI Assisted): describe your team and needs; the AI suggests matches you can invite.
- Find a Team (AI Assisted): describe yourself and your interests; request to join.
- Join a Team: pick a team, click the plus icon on an open spot, send a short message to the Team Lead.

Leave a team any time with the red "Leave team" link.

Team size: 1 to 5 people. Solo is fine.

Plan to communicate with your team outside the IBM site (Discord works).

## Slide 16

BAZ AI CODE REVIEW (2 minutes)

Baz is an AI code reviewer installed on the A2Tech360 GitHub org for this hackathon. Any pull request opened in an org repo gets an automatic review: likely bugs, risky changes, and suggestions, right in the PR.

Workflow:
1. Create a branch: git checkout -b feature/your-idea
2. Commit and push your work: git push -u origin feature/your-idea
3. Open a pull request into main
4. Read Baz's comments, fix what matters, push again; Baz re-reviews
5. Merge small PRs often so main is always demo-able

Why it matters: fewer demo-breaking bugs at 11:59 AM Sunday, a clean commit and PR history (judges verify code was written during hacking hours), and better code quality counts toward technical execution.

Baz is part of the Local Impact track's build requirements and available to every team.

Guide: docs/baz-code-review.md. Baz docs: https://docs.baz.co
