## Rules at a glance

1. All team members must register and check in.
2. Teams of 1–5 people. Solo is allowed.
3. All code is written during hacking hours (Sat 12:00 PM to Sun 12:00 PM). Brainstorming before is fine.
4. At least 40% of your code must be Jac.
5. One project per team.
6. AI tools, open-source libraries, and APIs are allowed, but the project must be original work created at the event.
7. At least one teammate must be present to demo.
8. Be respectful. The Code of Conduct is enforced; misrepresentation means disqualification.

## FAQ

**I've never written Jac.** That's expected. Go to the Jac workshop (Sat 2:00 PM), and start with the [Jac + Jaseci resources](../starter/).

**I don't have a team.** Solo is fine, and team matching happens in Discord and in the room.

**I don't code.** You're welcome here. Teams need people who talk to users, design, research the problem, and pitch.

**Do I have to use IBM or Google?** No. They're optional, and they make you eligible for Best of IBM and Best of Google.

**Can I use my Google Cloud credits next week?** No. Claim them during the event; unclaimed vouchers expire.
