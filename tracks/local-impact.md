# Local Impact Track

**Build something that makes life measurably better for people in Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County.**

a2Tech360 spends ten days celebrating what this region invents. This is the part where we invent something.

Every problem worth solving here already belongs to somebody. Riders waiting on a route that doesn't quite work. Small businesses competing against everything online. Nonprofits doing serious work on thin budgets. Students, newcomers, parents, makers, and longtime residents who could help each other if they knew how.

Pick one of theirs. Pick something we haven't thought of. **The only requirement is that a specific person in this county could use what you build on Monday morning.**

## A few directions, not a menu

- **Getting around.** Transit, parking, biking, and the last mile between where people are and where they need to be.
- **Main Street.** Helping local businesses reach the people already walking past them.
- **Showing up.** Connecting volunteers, mentors, and donors to the organizations that need them.
- **Access.** Food, housing, benefits, childcare, and the gap between a service existing and someone finding it.
- **Civic clarity.** Making local data, meetings, permits, and decisions legible to normal humans.
- **Belonging.** Helping people find each other and find what's happening.

## One rule

**Ground it in something real.** Talk to someone who has the problem. Judges will ask who you built this for, and "everyone" is the wrong answer.

You do not need to be a professional engineer. You do not need a team when you arrive. You do need to care about this place.

## Build requirements

- Build on the **Jaseci/Jac** stack (at least 40% of your code in Jac). No prior experience required; workshops and mentors are on site.
- You may incorporate **Google**, **IBM**, or **Baz** tooling alongside it.
- Local Impact projects use **Baz** for code review. Baz is installed on the A2Tech360 GitHub org: put your repo there and open pull requests ([guide](../docs/baz-code-review.md)).

## How to submit

Local Impact teams submit on **both** platforms:

- **IBM hackathon platform** ([ibm.biz/a2tech360](https://ibm.biz/a2tech360), Team Lead): written problem and solution statements; how you used IBM and/or Google technologies; your GitHub repo URL (preferably in the A2Tech360 org); a URL to a 1-minute recorded demo video
- **Devpost** ([jachacks-a2tech.devpost.com](https://jachacks-a2tech.devpost.com/)): like every JacHacks participant

Submissions are due **Sunday at 12:00 PM**. Presentations are 5 minutes: 3–4 minutes presenting plus 1–2 minutes of video demo. Full checklist: [SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md](../SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md).

## Prizes

- **Community Favorite:** every participant votes. This one belongs to the room.
- **Sponsor awards:** Best of IBM, Best of Google, and more, selected by each sponsor.

## Problem-discovery worksheet

Copy this into your project README and fill it in before you write much code. It becomes your written problem and solution statements, and the backbone of your 5-minute presentation.

```markdown
### Who
The specific person (role, neighborhood, situation) this is for:

### What we heard
Who did we talk to, and what did they tell us? (quotes are great)

### The problem, in their words


### What they do today (and why it falls short)


### Monday morning
What will this person be able to do on Monday that they can't do today?

### How we'll know it worked
One measurable signal (time saved, visits, sign-ups, dollars, trips):

### After the weekend
Who could own, host, or keep this running? (a nonprofit, a city office, a business, a student org)
```
